# Vettri HRMS: 30-Day SEO Content Strategy

This plan is for the existing Vettri HRMS marketing site. The articles should be reviewed and approved before publication. No article pages are published by this plan.

## Article 1: What Is HRMS Software? A Complete Guide for Businesses

- **SEO title:** What Is HRMS Software? A Complete Guide for Businesses | Vettri HRMS
- **Meta description:** Learn what HRMS software does, which HR workflows it supports, and how businesses can evaluate an HR management platform.
- **Target search intent:** Informational research from business owners and HR teams learning the HRMS category.
- **Suggested URL slug:** `/resources/what-is-hrms-software`
- **H1:** What Is HRMS Software? A Complete Guide for Businesses
- **H2 structure:**
  - What HRMS software means
  - Which HR workflows an HRMS can support
  - Employee records, attendance, leave and payroll context
  - How HRMS software supports workplace operations
  - When a business should consider an HRMS
  - Questions to ask before choosing HRMS software
- **Internal links:** `/platform`, `/solutions`, `/pricing`, `/contact`
- **CTA:** Explore Vettri HRMS for connected people and workplace operations.
- **Relevant Vettri pages:** Platform, Solutions, Pricing, Contact

## Publishing Sequence

1. **Days 1-7:** Publish Article 1 after subject-matter review; link to Platform and Solutions.
2. **Days 8-14:** Publish Article 2; link it from Article 1 and the Resources hub once the hub contains approved articles.
3. **Days 15-21:** Publish Article 3; connect it to the employee and HR operations sections of Platform.
4. **Days 22-30:** Publish Article 4; link it from Pricing, Product tour and Contact.

Before publication, each article should receive a unique canonical URL, Article schema only when the page contains the approved article, a descriptive OG image, one H1, and inclusion in the sitemap only after it is public and indexable.
